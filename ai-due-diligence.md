# AI due diligence platform for VC firms

**Role:** lead backend developer, plus parts of the React frontend · **Period:** Oct 2025 to present
**Stack:** Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Redis, React, and several LLM providers

## What it does

Before a VC firm invests, an analyst spends days researching a startup and writing an investment memo. This platform does most of that groundwork. An analyst uploads a pitch deck and gets back a researched, structured memo they can review, question and export.

## My part

- Designed and built the backend, including a system of 50+ AI agents and the job processing that runs them.
- Built the integrations with multiple LLM providers.
- Built the document handling, report export and real-time progress updates.
- On the frontend, built deal and task management screens and led the move to full internationalization.

The architecture and methods are proprietary, so this page stays at a high level. Happy to discuss my role in more depth in an interview.
